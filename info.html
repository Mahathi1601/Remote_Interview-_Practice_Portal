About the Portal

Welcome to the Remote Interview Practice Portal, a state-of-the-art interactive preparation platform designed to help candidates prepare for technical, behavioral, and screening interviews.

We leverage industry best practices and artificial intelligence to bridge the gap between candidate preparation and real-world hiring standards. Our core capabilities include:

  • Tailored Practice Sessions: Focus on core topics like Software Engineering, Product Management, System Design, and behavioral techniques.
  • AI Evaluation: Receive intelligent feedback on your answers to understand strengths, weaknesses, and optimization paths.
  • Dynamic Interview Planner: Structure your preparation journey with actionable timelines, checklists, and calendar reminders.
  • Comprehensive Analytics: Track metrics on performance over time to visualize score progression and subject mastery.

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy outlines how the Remote Interview Practice Portal collects, uses, and safeguards your personal and session details.

1. Information Collection: We collect your registration details (name, email, secure password hashes) and mock interview practice records (saved session scores and schedules) to personalize your preparation metrics.

2. Data Encryption: All sensitive profile details are encrypted in transit using standard Transport Layer Security (TLS) and stored securely inside database instances.

3. Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, distribute, or license your individual user accounts or performance logs to external marketing agencies.
